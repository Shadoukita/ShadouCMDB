### Added: Change histogram for an inventory query

`GET /api/v1/configuration-items/change-histogram` takes the filters of the inventory list
(`GET /api/v1/configuration-items`) plus `from`, `to` and `bucket` (`hour` or `day`), and returns the
changes to the matching CIs per UTC hour or day: `created`, `statusChanged` (an update that changed
the `status` attribute) and `updated` (every other update, deletion and restore), every bucket of the
range included, and a `total`. The counts come from the audit log, so the endpoint needs `audit.view`
and follows its visibility rules: a user limited to some classes counts only the CIs and entries
`GET /api/v1/audit-log` shows them. One request covers at most 7 days of hourly or 90 days of daily
buckets. The web UI will use it for the activity strip above the inventory table.
