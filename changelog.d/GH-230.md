### Security: first-run setup throttles wrong setup tokens and caps their log lines

While no user exists, every request to first-run setup (`POST /api/v1/setup`) with a wrong or
missing setup token was answered 403 and logged as a `WARN` line, without limit ([GH#230]). A client
that could reach a new installation could fill the log, and with size-based log rotation
(`--log-file`, `docker logs --log-opt max-size`) push out the line holding the generated setup
token. It could not guess the token.

- Wrong setup tokens are now throttled like wrong passwords: after 5 from one client network, setup
  is locked for that network for 1 s, 2 s, 4 s, ... up to 15 minutes, and 15 from several networks
  lock it for every network. While locked, setup answers 429 `RATE_LIMITED` with `Retry-After`,
  without checking the token. Once the first administrator exists, setup answers 409 as before and
  is never throttled.
- At most 10 refused setup requests per minute are logged one by one; the rest are counted and
  reported in one `WARN` line (`first-run setup refused N more times ...`).

If you are locked out of setup yourself, wait for the lock to expire, restart the server, or create
the first administrator with `shadoucmdb create-admin`.

**Upgrade:** nothing to do; no schema or configuration change.
