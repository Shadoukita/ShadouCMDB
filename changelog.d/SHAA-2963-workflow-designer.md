### Changed: the workflow designer in English and German

A workflow's Designer tab now follows the active language: the toolbar and save status, the Check panel, the
diagram's screen-reader labels, the state and transition inspectors, the condition editor with its comparisons,
the states and transitions tables, the delete, discard and publish confirmations, and the problems the designer
finds before saving. A transition's conditions read in the active language in the transitions table and in a
published version's read-only view on the Versions tab, for example "Umgebung ist Produktion und Risiko ist
höchstens 3". German users no longer see English text in the designer.

No URL, permission or API changes.
