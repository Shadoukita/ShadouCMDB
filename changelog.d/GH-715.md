### Security: A requester can no longer choose a field approver by staging the field on an earlier gated transition (GH#715)

A step whose approvers come from a CI field (approver source `ci_attribute`) did not use the field
when the requester had set it (GH#664). A requester could get around this by setting the field on
an earlier transition that needed approval: once that request was approved, the field change was
recorded as the approver's, so the next step's approvers were the ones the requester had chosen.

A field change applied by an approved request now also counts as a change by that request's
requester and by every user that request excluded. If any of them may not decide the new request,
the field is not used and it appears in `droppedSources` with reason `field_set_by_requester`.
`fieldLastChanged` gains `approvalRequestedBy`, listing those users. The approver preview with
`requestedBy` applies the same rule.

Upgrade: no migration. Requests already pending keep their approvers until an administrator's
**Refresh**, which applies the new rule.
