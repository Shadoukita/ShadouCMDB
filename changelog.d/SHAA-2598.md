### Fixed: Inventory filter chips and the change histogram under a "Needs attention" filter

On the inventory list, a filter chip with a long value no longer squeezes its key ("Class:", "Needs
attention:") until the two overprint. The key keeps its width; the value is cut off with an ellipsis
and shows in full on hover and to screen readers ([GH#787]).

Opened from a "Needs attention" check on the dashboard, the inventory's change histogram no longer
fails with an error. The histogram cannot be narrowed to a data-quality check, so the strip now says
so; remove the filter to see the changes ([GH#788]).
