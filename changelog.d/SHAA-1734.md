### Changed: Confirmations appear as notifications in the bottom-right corner

Messages such as "Created crm-app-01.", "Saved crm-app-01.", "Created user …", "Deleted group …" and "Archived class …" no longer appear as a banner above the page content. They now appear as notifications in the bottom-right corner, so the page no longer moves when one appears or closes. A notification stays when you navigate, so the confirmation for a record you just created still shows on its detail page.

Confirmations close after 6 seconds. The timer pauses while the pointer or keyboard focus is on the notification. Error notifications stay until you close them. Every notification has a close button, and **Escape** closes the focused one. **F8** moves keyboard focus to the newest notification, and after you close the last one, focus returns to where you were. Screen readers read confirmations politely and errors immediately.

Save confirmations shown inside a form or panel (for example on the account, import settings and customization pages) stay where they are.
