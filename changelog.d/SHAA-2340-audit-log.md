### Changed: Administration › Audit log in the new look

The audit log now has the same header as the inventory. The breadcrumb, the title with the entry
count, the introduction and the filters (actor name, record type, action and the source chips) sit on
one band above the table. Events are shown as pills, records that link to a CI, user or permission
profile are shown in a monospaced font in the link colour, and the page numbers sit in the footer of
the table.

No behaviour, URL or permission changes.
