### Added: Layout templates in the web UI, and layouts for a single CI

**Customization › Layouts** (the tab was called *Detail and form layout*) now has two tables:

- **Classes and their default layout**: every class with its default template, which you change in
  the row and save with the customization. Search by class or template name, filter by template and
  sort; the view is part of the address. **Edit CI…** opens the layout editor on one of the class's
  CIs, on its default template.
- **Layout templates**: each template with how many classes and CIs use it, and **Edit** (the layout
  editor on a CI that shows it), **Rename**, **Duplicate** and **Delete** (only when nobody uses it;
  otherwise it says who does), plus **New template** (blank or a copy of another one).

The **layout editor** says what it edits: *Template: <name> (used by N classes, M CIs)* or *This CI
only*. **Save to template "<name>"…** asks for confirmation and says how many classes and CIs the
change reaches. **More ways to save** offers **Save as new template…** (optionally the class's new
default) and **Save for this CI only**. On a CI, **Use template…** shows another template for that
CI and **Reset to class default** goes back to the class's template. **Save layout** and the note
field in the bar are gone: the note is asked for when saving to a template.

The detail page and the edit form of a CI show the CI's own layout or the template chosen for it.
Holders of `customization.manage` see a small **Own layout** or **Layout: <template>** marker on the
detail page of such a CI.

Layouts remain presentation only: hidden and read-only fields are still returned and accepted by the
API. No upgrade step; the templates and per-CI layouts come from migration 0042.
