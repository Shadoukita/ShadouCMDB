### Fixed: the notification action recipient cap no longer counts users who are not notified

`WORKFLOW_ACTIONS_MAX_RECIPIENTS` was applied to all the users an action names, in id order, before
the outbox left out inactive users and users who may not view the CI's type. Such users could use up
the cap, and recipients who should have been notified got nothing, with no delivery record, while the
action preview listed them as `included` ([GH#848]). The cap now counts only the users who are
notified, as the preview does. Users left out as inactive, `no_view`, the actor or already told by the
built-in notification are recorded as skipped and do not count. When the cap is reached, the run ends
`truncated` as before.

**Upgrade:** nothing to do.

[GH#848]: https://github.com/Shadoukita/ShadouCMDB/issues/848
