### Added: Web UI for linked Person records and the e-mail step at sign-in

The web UI now shows the link between sign-in accounts and Person CIs.

- **Administration › Users** lists each account's Person as a link. Accounts without an e-mail are
  marked **E-mail required**, and accounts that have an e-mail but no Person are marked **Account
  incomplete**. Those accounts can't sign in. A new **Sign-in state** filter is kept in the URL.
  E-mail is a required field on the user form, and on first-run setup. If the address is already
  taken by another account or another Person, the error appears next to the field. To link an
  incomplete account, save it again.
- A **Person** CI shows a **Sign-in account** panel with a link to the account. Only users with the
  `users.manage` permission see it. While an account is linked, the Person's Email is read-only on
  the form and shows "Managed by user <username>". The delete dialog explains that the Person can't
  be deleted until the account is disabled or deleted.
- An account created before e-mails were required is asked for its e-mail address right after
  sign-in, before anything else, including a required two-factor set-up. It then continues to the
  page it was opening.
- In the data model, the Person's Name and Email fields are marked **System**, and they can't be
  archived, made optional or retyped.
- A single sign-on that is refused because the account is incomplete now explains why on the sign-in
  page.
