### Added: Approvals in the workflow designer

Administrators can now set up approvals in **Administration › Workflows** instead of through the API.

- **Designer:** a transition's inspector has an **Approval** section. Add up to 5 steps. Each step has a
  name and key, the number of approvals it needs (1 to 20), an optional due period (15 minutes to 90
  days) and what happens when it is overdue (flag and escalate, or reject the request). Two flags control
  separation of duties: approvers of an earlier step may not approve this one, and decisions through an
  API token can be allowed. A step can also name the transitions whose actors on the CI may not approve
  it. The steps are saved with the draft and checked live, and the transitions table shows the policy. A
  draft that the check refuses cannot be published.
- **Approvers tab:** decide who may approve each step. The sources are a permission profile, a user
  group, a user, a Person field of the CI, or the business or technical owners of the CI's business
  services. Each source is either an approver or an escalation approver, who may decide only once the
  step is overdue. Assignments belong to the workflow, so changing them needs no new version. The check's
  warnings (no approvers, approvers who cannot view the type, too few for the quorum) show on their step.
- **Preview:** for a chosen step and CI, and optionally a requester, the server lists who would be asked
  to approve and why each user is in or out (eligible, only once overdue, excluded by four-eyes, cannot
  view the CI, account disabled). A CI you cannot view is not found, so the preview shows nothing beyond
  your own rights.
- **Publishing** a new or changed approval policy warns when instances are still running on older
  versions. They keep their version's rules until you migrate them on the Versions tab.

Approval policies and approvers are part of the configuration export (format 9) and come back on import.
Everything needs the **Manage workflows** permission, as before. All new text is available in English
and German.
