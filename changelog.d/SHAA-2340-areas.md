### Changed: Administration › Areas in the new look

The Areas page now has the same header as the inventory. The breadcrumb, the title with the area count,
New area, the introduction and the Show archived areas filter sit on one band above the areas. Area names
and the CI classes of each area are shown in a monospaced font in the link colour, and Active and Archived
are pills with a dot. The table uses the same headers as the other lists.

No behaviour, URL or permission changes.
