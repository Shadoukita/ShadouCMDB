### Changed: My account in the new look

The *My account* page now opens with the same head band as a CI page: the title, whether the account is an
Administrator, whether it signs in locally or through an identity provider, and the display name, username
and e-mail address. The password form shows the current, new and repeated password in one grid, with
*Change password* in the panel's footer. The title of freshly issued recovery codes is now set at the panel
title's size, and a failed copy to the clipboard is shown in the error colour with an icon.

No URL, permission, API or sign-in behaviour changes.
