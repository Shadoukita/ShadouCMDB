### Security: Dropped approver sources no longer show business service names to users who cannot view business services (GH#717)

When an approval step's service owners were not used for separation of duties, the request's
`droppedSources` named the business service, the owner and the user who changed them. Anyone who
could see the approval request saw these names, even without the right to view business services.

A user without the view right on business services now gets a general label (`technical owners of
the CI's business services`) and message, and `fieldLastChanged` without the actor or the users
behind the change. This applies to the approval request, its `workflow.approval_request` audit
entry and the approver preview. Users who may view business services still get the full names.
Dropped CI field sources are unchanged.
