### Added: "Needs attention" panel on the dashboard

The dashboard has a dark **Needs attention** panel beside the recent activity. It lists the
data-quality checks with the number of CIs each one finds, counting only CIs in classes you may
view:

- **Without an owner**: no value in the owner field of the CI's class.
- **End of life within 90 days**: end of life reached, or reached within 90 days.
- **No relationships**: not linked to any other CI you can see.
- **Pending approvals**: a workflow approval on the CI is waiting for a decision.

Select a check to open the inventory with only those CIs. The inventory shows the check as a
**Needs attention** filter chip that you can remove, and the address (`?quality=…`) can be
bookmarked or shared. The filter can be combined with the other inventory filters, but it cannot
be saved in a saved view.

The owner and end-of-life checks need a class setting that names the owner field and the
end-of-life field. A check is left out when no class you may view has that setting, so the panel
never shows a reassuring 0 for a check that cannot find anything. Users with `datamodel.manage`
see a note saying how many checks are off. Migration `0068` sets these fields where your classes
use conventional keys (see the data-quality entry).

In the built-in dashboard arrangement, the recent activity now takes two thirds of its row beside
the panel, and the status counts move to their own full-width row. Customized dashboards show the
panel beside their first **Recently changed** widget, or at the end when they have none.
