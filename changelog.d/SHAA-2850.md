### Security: Workflow approvers and action recipients no longer reveal users and groups to administrators who may not look them up

Looking up users and groups (`GET /api/v1/principals`) needs the edit permission on business
services or `users.manage`. Workflow administrators with `workflows.manage` but neither of those
could still learn which users and groups exist through the workflow designer. That is closed:

- Approver assignments (`PUT /api/v1/admin/workflow-definitions/{id}/approvers`) and action
  recipients (`PUT .../actions`) accept a user or group **by name** only from a caller who may look
  them up. Anyone else gets 400 `directory_lookup_forbidden` on that field, whether or not the name
  exists. Users and groups given by id, and those the workflow already has under that name, are
  still accepted, so saving an unchanged list keeps working. Permission profiles by name are not
  affected.
- The approver preview (`GET .../approvers/preview`) and the action preview
  (`GET .../actions/{key}/preview`) give such a caller the counts only: `users` is empty and the new
  field `usersHidden` is `true`.
- In the workflow designer, such an administrator no longer gets a name field for users and groups
  under **Approvers**. A note says which permission is needed, and the preview shows the counts
  with a note in place of the user list.

**Upgrade:** administrators who manage workflows but should also pick individual users or groups
as approvers or recipients need the `users.manage` permission or the edit permission on business
services. Without it they can assign permission profiles, or users and groups by id.
