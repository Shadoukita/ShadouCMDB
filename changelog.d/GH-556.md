### Security: a signed-in request's body must arrive within 30 seconds

A signed-in request takes its `HTTP_MAX_CONCURRENT_REQUESTS` slot before its body arrives, and the
body could take up to `HTTP_REQUEST_TIMEOUT_SECS` (120 s by default). Since one user holds at most a
quarter of the slots ([GH#502]), four accounts without any permission could send bodies slowly and
hold every slot, so every other user got `503 SERVER_BUSY` for each timeout window ([GH#556]).

The body of a signed-in request must now arrive within the new `HTTP_BODY_TIMEOUT_SECS` (default 30,
1 to 3600), or the request is answered `408 REQUEST_TIMEOUT` and its slot is given back. Requests
whose body arrives in one go, as from the web UI or a script on a normal link, are not affected.
Two routes keep their longer deadline: configuration import (up to 16 MiB, needs
`config.export_import`) keeps `HTTP_REQUEST_TIMEOUT_SECS`, and the bulk import file upload keeps
`IMPORT_UPLOAD_TIMEOUT_SECS`.

**Upgrade note:** no action needed. Raise `HTTP_BODY_TIMEOUT_SECS` only if clients send 1 MiB
request bodies over links slower than about 35 KB/s.
