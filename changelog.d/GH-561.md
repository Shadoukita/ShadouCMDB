### Security: one client network can no longer hold every open connection

The limit on open connections added for [GH#557] was one shared pool. A single client that reached
the server directly could fill it, for example with connections that never finish their request
headers, and every other client was refused, load-balancer health checks included ([GH#561]).

One client network (IPv4 /24, IPv6 /64) now holds at most a quarter of the open connections, and
one wider network (IPv4 /16, IPv6 /48) at most half. Connections past that share are closed at once,
and the warning `connections refused: one client network's share of the open connection limit
reached` is logged at most every 10 seconds. Peers listed in `TRUSTED_PROXIES` are not held to a
share, because behind a reverse proxy every connection comes from the proxy. The shares in effect
are logged at start-up on the `connection limit` line (`per_network`, `per_wide_network`).

**Upgrade:** when clients reach the server through a reverse proxy or load balancer, list it in
`TRUSTED_PROXIES` (you should already, for sign-in throttling and the audit trail), or all clients
share one quarter of the connections. No API or schema change.
