### Fixed: Workflow designer layout at laptop widths (GH#644)

**Arrange** now leaves enough room between states for a transition's name, and two transitions
between the same pair of states, one in each direction, run further apart, so their labels no
longer collide with each other or with the state boxes. Transition names longer than 22
characters are shortened in the diagram, and the full name of a state or transition shows on
hover. Arrows are drawn above the state boxes, so a label stays readable when a box is dragged
close to it. Diagrams saved with an earlier layout keep their positions until you click
**Arrange**.

When the designer is narrower than about 1,180 px (for example at 1280 or 1440 px screen width),
the inspector now sits under the diagram, and the diagram takes the full width. When some states
are out of view, the canvas edge is shaded and a note says that the diagram scrolls.

In the **States** and **Transitions** tables, long names wrap instead of running into the next
column, a transition's key is separated from its name, and **State value** shows the value's
name, as the inspector does, instead of its key. The diagram shows the value's name too.
