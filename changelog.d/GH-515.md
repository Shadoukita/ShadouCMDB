### Fixed: the upgrade's removal of membership-type relationship rules is in the audit log

Migration 0041 deleted the relationship rules on the built-in business service membership type
without an audit entry, so each removed rule's history ended at its `create` ([GH#515]). The
migration now records one `delete` per removed rule in the hash-chained audit log, in the same
transaction as the deletion: `entityType` relationship_type_rules, `actorType` system, `actorName`
`migration 0041`, `oldValue` the rule as `GET /relationship-rules/{id}` showed it, and `newValue`
holding the `reason` and `migration` 0041. `shadoucmdb audit-verify` verifies the chain through
them.

**Upgrade:** nothing to do. A database that already ran a pre-release build of migration 0041 is
accepted as is (its checksum is recorded as superseded); the rules it removed have no audit entry.

[GH#515]: https://github.com/Shadoukita/ShadouCMDB/issues/515
