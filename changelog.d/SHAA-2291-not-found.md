### Changed: The "Page not found" screen in the new look

An address that matches no screen now opens with the same head band as other pages: the title, an
*Error 404* badge and the address that was requested, in a monospaced font. Below it, a panel explains
what happened and offers *Go to the dashboard* and *Open the inventory*. The texts are available in
English and German.

No URL, permission or API behaviour changes.
