### Added: Keyboard navigation in the inventory and search lists

- In the inventory and the search results, the up and down arrow keys move between rows, and Enter opens the configuration item. In the inventory, **E** opens the focused item for editing (when you may edit it) and **C** opens the column chooser. The shortcuts do nothing while you type in a field.
- The row that has keyboard focus is highlighted. On screens wider than 820 px, the available shortcuts are listed under the table.
- A sortable column that is not sorted now shows a neutral sort icon when you point at or focus its header, so sortable columns can be told apart from the others.
- The **View** menu, the save, rename, copy and share dialogs, and **Manage views** are now available in German.
