### Added: Counts for saved views and workflows in the navigation rail and on a CI's Workflows tab

- Each saved view in the rail shows how many CIs it lists for you. Past 10,000 the figure reads
  `10,000+`. A view that could not be counted in time shows a dash and is counted again on your
  next page change. Hover over the figure to see what it means.
- **Workflows** in the rail shows the running workflow instances on the CIs you may view. A
  highlighted badge counts the approvals waiting for your decision; this badge also shows on the
  collapsed rail.
- A CI's **Workflows** tab counts that CI's running instances.

The counts load once and refresh when you move to another page, at most every 30 seconds. They do
not poll in the background. No configuration or migration is needed.
