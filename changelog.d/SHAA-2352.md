### Added: Count endpoints for saved views and open workflow work

Two read-only endpoints supply the counts in the navigation rail and on a CI's Workflows tab:

- `GET /api/v1/saved-views/counts` (`countSavedViews`, session only) returns how many CIs each of
  the caller's saved views shows, counted as the inventory list or global search would count them
  for that user, within their class rights. Counts stop at 10,000 (`at_least`). All counts of one
  request share a 2-second allowance; a view not counted in time is `timed_out` instead of slowing
  the page. At most 50 views per request.
- `GET /api/v1/workflow-instances/counts` (`countWorkflowInstances`) returns the running workflow
  instances, how many of them await approval, and the size of the caller's approval inbox, on the
  CIs the caller may view, or with `ciId` on one CI (404 for a CI the caller may not view).

Neither endpoint writes or audits anything, and no migration is needed.
