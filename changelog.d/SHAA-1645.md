### Changed: Layout editor: Record and Relationships are windows; separators between fields

In the layout editor (Edit layout on a CI page) the record details (ID, class, created and last
changed) and the relationships are windows like the other sections: move them to any tab, resize
them, or remove them. A removed panel is no longer shown on the CI's page; **+ Panel** adds it back.
A new or built-in layout starts with both at the end of its first tab, as the page has always shown
them. The record details are no longer pinned to the end of the first tab.

Field sections can hold separators: **+ Separator** in a section's toolbar adds a line across the
section with an optional label. Move it like a field (drag it, or Alt+↑ / Alt+↓ on its grip), edit
its label by clicking it, and remove it from its toolbar or with Delete. The CI page and the form
show it as a line across the section with its label.
