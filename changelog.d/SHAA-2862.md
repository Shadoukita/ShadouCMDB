### Added: Send a test on the workflow Notifications tab

Administration › Workflows › Notifications has a new **Send a test** panel. Pick a saved notification and
send it once, to yourself only. An e-mail goes to your own address (optionally about a CI you choose), a
webhook gets one signed `ping`, and an inbox notification appears in your own notifications. The panel says
where the test went and how long it took. If the relay or the receiver refuses it, the panel shows the
reason and the SMTP or HTTP status.

When outbound e-mail is off (`MAIL=off`) or webhooks are off (`WEBHOOKS_ALLOWED` not `true`), the panel says
so in place and names the setting the operator must change. Nothing is sent in that case. Tests use the
saved notifications, so save your changes first. Each test is recorded in the audit log.
