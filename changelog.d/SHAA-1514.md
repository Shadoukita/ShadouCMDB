### Added: Which CIs have a layout of their own, in Customization › Layouts and the inventory

- **Customization › Layouts**, classes table: a sortable column **CIs with their own layout** counts
  the live CIs of the class itself (not its subclasses) that show their own layout or another
  template. The number links to those CIs in the inventory. It shows **?** when you may not view the
  class.
- **Layout templates › Edit** opens the layout editor on a CI that shows the template as its own
  layout when there is one, so a template that only individual CIs use opens on one of them.
- **Inventory**: the `ownLayout=true|false` and `layoutTemplate=<key>` address parameters filter the
  list and show as removable filters. A saved view cannot hold them; saving one with these filters
  set is refused with an explanation.
