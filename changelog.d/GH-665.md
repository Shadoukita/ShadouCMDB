### Security: connections that send no request no longer hold a place under the connection limit

`HTTP_HEADER_READ_TIMEOUT_SECS` applied only once a connection was known to be HTTP/1. A client
that connected and sent nothing, sent part of the HTTP/2 connection preface, or opened an HTTP/2
connection without a request was never disconnected, and each such connection kept its place under
the open connection limit. A few client networks could take every place without signing in, and
the server then refused every other connection ([GH#665]).

A connection that has no request in progress for `HTTP_HEADER_READ_TIMEOUT_SECS` (default 10 s),
counted from when it was accepted or from the end of its last response, is now closed whatever its
protocol; an HTTP/2 client is sent a `GOAWAY` first. A request in progress is never cut by this
limit, however long its response takes. HTTP/2 clients reconnect when they need to, as HTTP/1
clients already did after the same idle time. No configuration, API or schema change.

[GH#665]: https://github.com/Shadoukita/ShadouCMDB/issues/665
