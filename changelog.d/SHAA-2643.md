### Added: Approval delegation and deadlines for approval steps

- **Delegation.** A user can let a deputy decide their approvals for a time, for example during
  leave: `POST /api/v1/me/approval-delegations` with `delegateUserId`, `startsAt` (may be in the
  future), `endsAt` (required, at most 90 days later) and optionally `definitionKey` to limit it to
  one workflow. `GET /api/v1/me/approval-delegations` lists the delegations of your approvals and
  those to you; the principal or the delegate revokes one with `POST …/{id}/revoke`. Delegations
  are never deleted, only revoked, and at most 5 can be scheduled or active per user.
- A delegation applies only within its window, while it is not revoked and both accounts are
  active. It lends only the principal's own approvals, never ones the principal holds as someone
  else's delegate, and never visibility: the delegate decides only requests on CIs that both may
  view. Four-eyes binds the delegate as it binds the principal: no one approves, in person or for
  someone, a request they or their principal made. A delegate who could decide for several people
  says for whom (`onBehalfOf` on the decision; `myEligibility.onBehalfOf` lists the choices). One
  vote per person per step holds across in-person and delegated votes. The decision, its event
  (`onBehalfOfName`) and its audit row (`onBehalfOf`, `delegationId`) record who acted for whom.
  The approvals inbox lists the requests you may decide for someone.
- **Absences.** Holders of `users.manage` delegate for someone who is absent unexpectedly with
  `POST /api/v1/admin/approval-delegations` (`principalUserId`, …), list them with `GET` and revoke
  them with `POST …/{id}/revoke`. An administrator can never name themselves as the delegate: the
  API refuses it with 400 and the database refuses such a row. The principal sees the delegation
  in their own list, with its creator.
- Creating and revoking delegations needs a signed-in session (API tokens are refused) and is
  audited as `create` and `update` on `workflow_approval_delegations`, users by name. Auditors whose
  profile limits the types they may view see the workflow a delegation is limited to only when they
  may view its type.
- **Deadlines.** Each server process now runs the approval SLA sweep. An approval step past its due
  date is marked overdue (`overdue: true`), with an `approval_overdue` event and a
  `workflow.approval_overdue` audit row by `system`. Its escalation approvers may decide it from
  then on, not before. A step set to `onOverdue: reject` closes its request as rejected with close
  reason `overdue`, and the instance stays in its state. A step that too few users can decide is
  flagged once (`reason: understaffed`). The sweep never approves anything.
- Changing a workflow's approvers (`PUT …/approvers`) now resolves the active steps of its pending
  requests again right away, audited as `workflow.approval_refresh` by `system` when a step's
  approvers change; the sweep finishes what is left.
- Several server processes on one database may all run the sweep: each overdue step gets exactly one
  event and one audit row.

**Upgrade:** no migration and nothing to do. The sweep is on by default and runs every 60 seconds.
Two new optional settings control it per server process: `WORKFLOW_APPROVAL_SWEEP=off` turns it
off in that process (keep it on in at least one), and `WORKFLOW_APPROVAL_SWEEP_INTERVAL_SECS` sets
the interval (10 to 3600). See `.env.example`.
