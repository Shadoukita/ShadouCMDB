### Security: Impact analysis stays within IMPACT_TIMEOUT_MS plus a fixed 2 s

After `IMPACT_TIMEOUT_MS`, an impact analysis (`GET /api/v1/configuration-items/{id}/impact` and
its CSV export) ran several more queries that each had 2 s of their own. On a very slow database,
an analysis could hold its database connection and its concurrency places for
`IMPACT_TIMEOUT_MS` + 6 s or more, and run into `HTTP_REQUEST_TIMEOUT_SECS` instead of answering
its truncated result (GH#393). The walks now stop at `IMPACT_TIMEOUT_MS` counted from the start of
the request, and every query that assembles the result shares one allowance of 2 s after that. A
result that cannot be assembled in that time is answered `503 SERVER_BUSY` with `Retry-After`.

**Upgrade:** the startup check now requires `IMPACT_TIMEOUT_MS` + 2000 to be below
`HTTP_REQUEST_TIMEOUT_SECS` × 1000. The defaults (5000 ms and 120 s) pass. A server with, for
example, `HTTP_REQUEST_TIMEOUT_SECS=5` and `IMPACT_TIMEOUT_MS=4000` no longer starts; lower
`IMPACT_TIMEOUT_MS` or raise `HTTP_REQUEST_TIMEOUT_SECS`.
