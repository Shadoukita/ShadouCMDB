### Security: workflow conflicts no longer name workflows the manager may not view

A user with `workflows.manage` limited to some CI types gets "not found" for a workflow on types they
may not all view. The checks that refuse a workflow change because of another workflow still named
that other workflow, and its transition, in the error message. This happened when activating a
workflow whose state field another workflow drives or takes in a transition, when activating a
workflow whose transition takes another workflow's state field, and in the draft check and publish
of such a workflow (also through the configuration import). Only workflow and transition keys were
disclosed, no CI data. ([GH#718])

The refusal and its error code are unchanged (`state_attribute_driven`, `state_field_in_transition`,
`state_field`). When the other workflow is hidden from the caller, the message now says "another
workflow on these CIs" instead of its key; an administrator who may view it still sees its name.
