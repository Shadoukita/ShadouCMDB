### Fixed: delete confirmation opened from a table row is centred and shows its full text

The **Delete** confirmation on Relationship types, Dropdowns and Lookups rows opened at the left
edge of the window, over the sidebar, with right-aligned lines cut off at the dialog edge. Users
who may not view every CI type could not read why a row in use cannot be deleted ("number
withheld") or the advice to archive it instead. The dialog is now centred and wraps its text like
every other confirmation.
