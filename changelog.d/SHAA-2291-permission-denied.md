### Changed: The "Permission denied" screen in the new look

A screen you may not open now looks the same everywhere: in Administration, Imports, Business services
and on a configuration item's page and edit page. A head band shows *Permission denied*, an *Error 403*
badge and what is missing. A global permission is shown by name with its key in a monospaced font, for
example *Manage users* `users.manage`. A class right is shown in words, for example *Needs the Edit right
on Application*. Below it, a panel explains the refusal and links back to the inventory, the record or the
dashboard. The texts are available in English and German. The configuration item pages were English only
before.

No URL, permission or API behaviour changes.
