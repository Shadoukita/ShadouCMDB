### Security: changing a business service's class no longer tells whether it has hidden members

`PATCH /api/v1/configuration-items/{id}` with a new `classId` on a business service was refused by
the database only when the service had members or owners, and the refusal named the constraint.
Combined with a `validUntil` equal to the stored `validFrom`, a user who may not view some member
classes could learn, without changing anything, whether a service had members hidden from them
([GH#409], CWE-203).

- A business service now never changes its class, and no configuration item moves into the
  business service class: `400` with `business_service_class` on `classId`, the same answer for
  every service whatever its members and owners. To retype one, create a new item instead.
- The validity period of an update is checked against the stored dates the request leaves
  unchanged: `400` on `validUntil` (or `validFrom`) instead of a database constraint error.

No database change.

[GH#409]: https://github.com/Shadoukita/ShadouCMDB/issues/409
