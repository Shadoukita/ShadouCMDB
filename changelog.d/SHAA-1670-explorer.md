### Changed: Inventory and search lists are easier to scan

- The column names of the inventory and the search results now stay in view while you scroll through a long page of results (on screens wider than 820 px).
- Each row has an actions menu (⋯) with **Open** and **Impact analysis**, instead of an Impact button on every row.
- The class column shows the class icon next to the class name.
- Filters that come from a link or a list view (lookup values, IP network, layout) appear as removable chips under the toolbar.
- The toolbar uses one label style, and the search box has a search icon.
- The search page has its own search box. In the **Matched on** column, the matching text is highlighted.
- While a list loads, placeholder rows show where the rows will appear.
