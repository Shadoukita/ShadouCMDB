### Changed: Dashboard with KPI cards, period switch, changes chart and recent activity

The dashboard opens with the date and a greeting. A switch at the top right chooses the period:
**24 h**, **14 days** (the default) or **90 days**. The choice is part of the address
(`?period=24h`, `?period=90d`), so it survives a reload and can be bookmarked or shared.

Four cards show the key figures:

- **Configuration items** and **Relationships**: the count now, a chip with the change over the
  period, and bars with the count per day (per week for 90 days). For 24 h the chip shows the
  change since midnight UTC, because the counts are kept per UTC day.
- **Changes this period**: changes to CIs in the period, with the change in percent compared with
  the period before. Shown with the `audit.view` permission.
- **Records complete**: the share of CIs that have a value in every required and expected field,
  from the completeness figures added in this release.

Below the cards, **Changes over …** shows the changes per hour or day as stacked bars, split into
updated, created and status changed. **Show values** lists every count as a table. **CIs by class**
shows one bar per class, largest first, with the count and a link to the inventory. Hover over a
count, or use a screen reader, to get its share of all CIs.

The built-in **Recently changed** widget now shows the recent activity from the audit log: the CI,
its class, which fields changed, who changed it and when, plus a link to the audit log. Users
without `audit.view` still see the most recently updated CIs. Customized dashboards keep their
widgets and render in the new style. In the built-in arrangement, the recent activity is now
beside the status counts. The **Open inventory** and **New CI** buttons are no longer in the page
header: **New CI** is in the top bar, and **Open inventory** is on the **CIs by class** card.
