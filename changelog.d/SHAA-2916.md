### Added: approve and reject workflow transitions in the web UI

A transition that needs approval now says so in its dialog, lists its approval steps, and submits as
**Request approval**. While a request is pending, the CI's Workflows tab and the workflow instance page show a
banner with the active step's progress, its due date (marked when overdue) and who asked. It offers
**Review and decide** to approvers, **View request** to everyone else, and **Withdraw request** to the requester.

The decision dialog approves or rejects (a rejection needs a reason) and lets a delegate choose whom they
decide for. Anyone who may not decide sees why in plain language, for example the four-eyes rule for the
requester, and gets no approve button. A request that can no longer be applied because the CI changed says that
nothing was recorded and what to do instead. The workflow instance page lists every approval request with its
steps and decisions. No API or database change.
