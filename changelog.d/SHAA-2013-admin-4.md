### Changed: Customization, identity providers and the layout editor

- **Identity providers:** the list has one toolbar and the order hint, and each row opens from its **More actions** (`⋯`) menu. The edit page starts with a title row, keeps Delete in its `⋯` menu and saves with the bar at the bottom of the window. Saving, enabling and disabling are confirmed by a notification.
- **Customization:** Save, Discard and the comment for the new version are in the bar at the bottom of the window, as on the other edit pages. "Saved as version N" is a notification.
- **Branding preview:** the preview now shows the menu rail and the sign-in card, in the light and the dark theme, with your colours, name and logo. The colour pickers start at the built-in colours of the current theme.
- **Layout editor:** the editing bar is calmer: icons on Undo and Redo, a Snap toggle that shows when it is on, and the unsaved state as on the other save bars. Placed fields and separators have solid borders; dashed borders now only mark what the layout does not place. The mouse and keyboard help is a collapsible list under the canvas.
- **German:** the Customization page frame, Branding, the layout editor bar and the identity provider pages are available in German.
