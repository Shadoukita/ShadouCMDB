### Changed: Administration › Groups in the new look

The Groups list now has the same header as the inventory. The breadcrumb, the title with the
group count, **Create group**, the introduction and the search sit on one band above the table.
Group names are shown in a monospaced font in the link colour, and the page numbers sit in the
footer of the table.

A group's page has the same header as a CI page. It shows the breadcrumb, a group icon on a tile,
the group name, the member count and when the group was last updated. In the members table,
usernames are shown in a monospaced font, the status is a pill with a dot (**Active**,
**Disabled**), and the page numbers sit in the footer. The actions are unchanged.

No behaviour, URL or permission changes.
