### Added: optional audit of every workflow action delivery (`AUDIT_WORKFLOW_DELIVERIES`)

Operators whose audit duties require proof of each notification can now set
`AUDIT_WORKFLOW_DELIVERIES=true` (default `false`, GH#882). Every e-mail and webhook delivery that ends
`delivered` or `skipped` then writes one `workflow.action_delivery` audit entry (actor `system`, on the
delivery) in the same transaction that records the outcome: the run, action, kind, CI, recipient (a
user's id, an endpoint's id, or a fixed address masked as `c***@corp.example`), status and reason. The
message and full addresses are never audited. In-app notifications are not covered: the notification
is its own record.

- Off, nothing changes: only deliveries that give up (`workflow.action_dead`) and administrator retries
  and discards are audited.
- `workflow.action_dead` entries now also name the recipient, in the same form.
- Migrations 0082 and 0083 add the action to the audit log's checks (validated in a separate
  transaction, so the log is never locked for writes) and to the `changes` scope of
  `shadoucmdb prune-audit`.
- The audit log page shows the new entries as "Workflow action delivery".
