### Added: Facet counts for the inventory list

`GET /api/v1/configuration-items/facets` takes the filters of the inventory list
(`GET /api/v1/configuration-items`) and returns how many CIs match each facet value: per class,
criticality, each lookup list used by a lookup field (status, environment, location, ...) and, when
business services are configured, per business service (direct members). It also returns `total`,
the number of CIs that match every filter.

- **Multi-select counts.** Each facet is counted with its own filter left out and every other filter
  applied. Selecting a second class therefore still shows the counts of the other classes.
- **Permissions.** The counts include only CIs in classes the caller may view, the same as the list. A
  business service the caller may not view is not returned as a value.
- **Size.** `valueLimit` (default 50, at most 200) caps the values per facet, largest counts first.
  Selected values are always returned. `truncated` reports when values were left out. Counts are exact.
- **New list filter.** `businessServiceId` (comma-separated service CI ids) limits the inventory list,
  global search and the facets to the direct members of those services.

**Upgrade:** no migration.
