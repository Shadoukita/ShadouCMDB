### Security: plain-named session cookies ignored behind HTTPS; `__Host-` sign-in flow cookies

Behind HTTPS the server now reads the session only from `__Host-shadoucmdb_session` ([GH#285]). A
`shadoucmdb_session` cookie, which another host under the same domain can set with `Domain=`, is ignored
there instead of being accepted and moved to the `__Host-` name, so such a host can no longer sign a
visitor in to an account it controls. The cookies of a sign-in in progress are renamed the same way
behind HTTPS: `__Host-shadoucmdb_mfa` (second factor) and `__Host-shadoucmdb_oidc` (OIDC), both now
with `Path=/`. Plain HTTP setups keep the plain names.

**Upgrade:** behind HTTPS, anyone still signed in under the plain cookie name (a session from before
`__Host-` cookies) is asked to sign in again, and a sign-in that was waiting for its second factor or
for the OIDC provider during the upgrade starts over. Do not rewrite cookie names, `Path` or `Domain`
at the reverse proxy.
