### Added: Workflow actions and notifications in the designer

*Administration → Workflows → Designer*: a transition's inspector has a **Rules** tab and an
**Actions** tab. Under **Set fields**, the Actions tab lists the CI fields the transition sets when
it runs. A field can be set to a value, today's date, the current date and time, the person running
the transition, or cleared. These attribute actions are part of the draft: they are badged *Takes
effect when published*, and the draft check reports refused targets on their row (the state field,
identifying and read-only fields, fields the transition asks for, references to anything but a
Person). The tab also lists the workflow's notifications for the transition and links to the
**Notifications** tab, where they are managed.

A new **Notifications** tab on the workflow edits its inbox, e-mail and webhook actions: the
trigger (a transition, an approval being requested, starting a step, closing or going overdue, an
instance being cancelled or forced), the recipient sources, the content level, the subject and
intro in English and German, and for webhooks the endpoint and the CI fields in the payload.
Notifications belong to the workflow rather than a version. They are saved together with their own
Save button and apply at once. The API's refusals and the lint's warnings appear next to the field
they are about. **Who would be notified** resolves a saved notification's recipients for a chosen
CI, without sending anything.

This release of the server delivers inbox notifications only, to profiles, groups and named users.
Until the e-mail and webhook releases ship, it refuses e-mail and webhook actions and the other
recipient sources, and the refusal appears on the field.
