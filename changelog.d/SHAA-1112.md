### Security: "Part of" business services stays within IMPACT_TIMEOUT_MS plus a fixed 2 s

`GET /api/v1/configuration-items/{id}/business-services` walks membership with the impact analysis
engine, so its walk already stopped at `IMPACT_TIMEOUT_MS`. The queries that ran after the walk
(the services found and their owners) had no time limit of their own: on a very slow database the
request could hold its database connection and its impact analysis place until
`HTTP_REQUEST_TIMEOUT_SECS`. Every query of the request now ends within the same 2 s allowance after
`IMPACT_TIMEOUT_MS` that impact analysis uses, and a list that cannot be assembled in that time is
answered `503 SERVER_BUSY` with `Retry-After`. No configuration change is needed.
