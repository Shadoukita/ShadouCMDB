### Added: Saved views in the inventory and search

The inventory (*Configuration items* and every class list) and the search page have a **View** menu at
the top of the list. It saves the list as you see it, under a name: the classes, filters, sort, columns
and page size of the inventory, or the search term and filters of a search.

- **Switch, save, rename, delete.** The menu lists *My views* and *Shared views*. When the list
  differs from the view you opened, a **Modified** marker appears with **Save** and **Revert**. From
  the menu you can also save the list as a new view, rename the current view or delete it. **Manage
  views…** lists all your views in a table, for both the inventory and search.
- **Default view per list.** *Set as my default for Server* opens the Server list with that view from
  then on. *Clear my default* removes it, and the list opens with the class's list view from
  *Customization* again. Search views cannot be a default.
- **Shared views.** Users with the new right `views.share` can tick *Share with everyone* when they
  save a view, or use *Share a copy…* on a personal view. Everyone else can open a shared view, copy it
  to their own views, and make it their default.
- **Links keep working.** The address holds the view and all of its settings. A reload, a bookmark or
  *Copy link* shows the same list, also to a colleague who cannot open your personal view.
- **Changed data model.** A view whose column or class was archived opens with a notice that names
  what was left out. *Save to fix* stores the view without it. A view whose filter no longer exists is
  greyed out in the menu and is never applied, so it never lists more than it used to.
- The *Columns* button moves next to the View menu.
