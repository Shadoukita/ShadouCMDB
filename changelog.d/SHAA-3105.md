### Added: Change (RFC) template for new workflows

**Administration › Workflows › New workflow** offers a third starting point, **Change (RFC → assess
→ CAB → implement → review)**. It creates the states Requested (initial), Assessment, Approved,
Implementation, Review, Closed (final) and Rejected (final), with the transitions Submit for
assessment, Approve (CAB), Reject (comment required), Start implementation, Implementation
complete, Close and Cancel request. Approve (CAB) carries a *CAB approval* step needing one
approval; whoever submitted the change for assessment may not approve it, and four-eyes applies
as always. The CAB's approvers are not chosen by the template: the form says so, and the designer's
check warns that nobody may approve the step (and now points to the **Approvers** tab) until they
are assigned. The result is an ordinary draft that is edited, published, exported and imported
like any other workflow; if a step fails after the workflow was created, it is deleted again.
While a CI is in the Assessment state (key `assessment`), its **Workflows** tab links to the CI's
**Impact** tab.
