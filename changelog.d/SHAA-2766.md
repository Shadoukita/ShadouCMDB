### Fixed: Impact analysis on a slow database answers 503 instead of 500

When the database was slow and an impact analysis
(`GET /api/v1/configuration-items/{id}/impact`) or a CI's business services
(`GET /api/v1/configuration-items/{id}/business-services`) was near the end of its time, the
request could fail with `500 INTERNAL_ERROR` and log `unhandled database error` (canceling
statement due to statement timeout). The time limit of one query also applied to the statements
after it. These cases now answer `503 SERVER_BUSY` with `Retry-After: 1`, as other slow analyses
already did. Each step of the analysis now has only its own time limit, so an analysis that still
has time can finish and answer its result.
