### Changed: CI detail page head, tab counts, grouped relationships and history timeline

The CI page has a new header: the class icon on a tinted tile, the CI name in a monospaced font,
the class, state and criticality as chips, and the ident with the last update. With the
`audit.view` permission the header also says who made the last change (for example *Updated
5 min ago by J. Okafor*). The tabs show counts: relationships on **Relationship map**, workflow
instances on **Workflows**, and entries on **History**. The summary tiles under the title were
removed; the tab counts show the same numbers.

When a class has no layout of its own, the **Overview** puts all field sections in one card
beside the relationships, with the five newest history entries under them. **Full history** opens
the History tab. Layouts configured under Customization show their tabs and windows as before,
in the new panel style. Saved layouts and layout templates need no change.

Relationships are grouped by how they read from this CI (for example *Is located in* or
*Hosts*). Each row shows the related CI's class icon, its name as a link, its class and notes,
and a pill with the direction and the relationship type's key. **All / Outgoing / Incoming**
and a text filter narrow the list. Remove has moved into each row's **⋯** menu.

The history, on CI and business service pages, is a timeline. Each change shows the old and new
values as struck-through and highlighted chips. Times stay in UTC, and the source filter and
paging work as before.
