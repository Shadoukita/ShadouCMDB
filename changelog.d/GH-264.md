### Security: the audit log no longer counts entries about CIs the caller may not view

For a user whose permission profile limits the classes they may view, `GET /api/v1/audit-log`
blanked the values of entries about CIs of other classes but still listed and counted them. With
the `entityId`, `requestId` or time filters, `page.total` told how many CIs of a hidden class a
purge had deleted, and the timestamps of a hidden CI's history ([GH#264]).

Those entries (about a CI of a class the caller may not view or that no longer exists, and
relationship entries with such an endpoint) are now left out of the page and of `page.total`
under every filter. Users who may view every class see no change. Schema change entries are
still listed, with the counts the caller may see ([GH#261]).
