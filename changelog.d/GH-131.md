### Security: OIDC sign-ins must prove MFA for profiles that require it

A permission profile with `requireMfa` exempted every OIDC account, on the assumption that the
provider runs the second factor. Nothing checked it, so a provider whose policy for ShadouCMDB was
password-only gave such a profile (up to Administrator) on one factor ([GH#131]).

OIDC providers have a new MFA setting. Existing providers are set to 'Trust the provider' (unchanged
behaviour: ShadouCMDB does not check that the provider used a second factor). Review each provider
and switch it to 'Verify' where your IdP sends `amr` (Microsoft Entra ID, Okta, Auth0, Ping) or a
configurable `acr` (Keycloak LoA). New providers default to 'Verify'. With 'Verify', users whose
profiles require MFA are refused when the ID token does not prove a second factor.

- **Verify** (`oidc.mfaAssurance: "verify"`): the signed ID token's `amr` must contain `mfa` or
  cover two factor categories, or, with `oidc.requiredAcr` set, its `acr` must be one of those
  values (they are also sent as `acr_values`). A refused sign-in ends at
  `/login?ssoError=mfa_not_enforced` and is audited as `login.failure` with
  `reason: "mfa_not_enforced"`.
- **Sessions already open** are re-checked on every request: when a profile starts to require MFA
  or a provider is switched to Verify, an OIDC session whose sign-in did not prove MFA ends
  (`401`, `session.revoke` with `reason: "mfa_not_enforced"`) and the user signs in again.
- **Audit:** `login.success` rows of OIDC sign-ins carry `providerMfa` (`verified`, `trusted` or
  `none`), and `login.failure` rows of refused provider sign-ins carry `reason`. The provider's
  connection test warns when discovery suggests the check cannot pass.

**Upgrade:** migration 0023 adds the setting and sets every existing OIDC provider to 'Trust the
provider', so nobody is locked out. After upgrading, review each provider as described in the
[hardening guide][hardening] and switch it to 'Verify' where possible; test with a
non-administrator account first and keep the local break-glass administrator at hand.
