### Fixed: business-service member exports no longer fail with 503 under concurrent activity

`GET /api/v1/business-services/{id}/members/export` read the members in one snapshot and wrote its
audit record in the same transaction. Any other audited event that committed while the export ran,
even a failed sign-in, made that audit write a serialization failure, and the export answered
`503 SERVER_BUSY` ([GH#514]). No export was ever delivered without its audit record. The members are
now read in a read-only snapshot and the audit record is written in its own transaction afterwards,
as the configuration export already does. No API or database change.

**Upgrade:** nothing to do.
