### Added: workflow action deliveries can be listed, retried and discarded, with a per-action summary

Administrators can now see what a workflow's notification actions delivered, and act on deliveries
that failed. All endpoints need `workflows.manage` and the view right on the workflow's types, like
the other workflow settings. Retrying and discarding also need the edit right on every one of those
types (403 FORBIDDEN otherwise), as for any other change to the workflow. Webhook deliveries are
listed and changed only with `webhooks.manage` as well.

- `GET /api/v1/admin/workflow-definitions/{id}/action-deliveries` lists the workflow's deliveries,
  newest first and paginated. Filter by `actionKey`, `status`, `kind`, `from`/`to` (when the run
  queued it), `instanceId`, `eventId` and `ciId`. Each delivery shows its status and reason, its
  attempts, when a pending one is due and the last status code. A fixed e-mail address is shown
  masked (`c***@corp.example`). A CI's label is shown only to callers who may view its type.
- `GET …/action-deliveries/{deliveryId}` adds the last error, the run's status and the event the
  delivery tells of.
- `POST …/action-deliveries/{deliveryId}/retry` sends a `dead` or `held` e-mail or webhook delivery
  again with fresh attempts. Its `WORKFLOW_ACTIONS_MAX_AGE_HOURS` counts from the retry, so a dead
  letter retried the next day is not expired again at once. `POST …/action-deliveries/{deliveryId}/discard`
  gives up on a `pending`, `held` or `dead` delivery: it gets the status `discarded` and is never
  sent. A delivery being sent can be neither retried nor discarded (409 CONFLICT `not_retryable`,
  `not_discardable`).
- `POST …/action-deliveries/retry` and `POST …/action-deliveries/discard` do the same for up to 1,000
  deliveries, given by `ids` or selected by a `filter`. The response lists the ids it changed, the ids
  it refused and why, and whether more deliveries match the filter.
- Each retried or discarded delivery writes one audit entry (`workflow.action_retry`,
  `workflow.action_discard`), with the signed-in user as actor, the status before and after, and the
  CI in `ciId`. The entry never records the recipient's address. These endpoints need a signed-in
  session; API tokens get 403.
- `GET /api/v1/admin/workflow-definitions/{id}/actions/summary` shows, per action, the deliveries of
  the last 24 hours and 7 days by status, the runs suppressed in the last 24 hours, and the age of the
  oldest pending delivery. It also shows the state of the whole queue: whether it is overloaded, the
  backlog, when the workers last counted it, and the limits in force.

**Upgrade:** migration 0076 adds a nullable column (`retried_at`) to `workflow_action_deliveries` and
two indexes; it rewrites no table.
