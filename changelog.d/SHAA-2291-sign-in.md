### Changed: Sign-in in the new look

The sign-in page is a centred card on the page background, in the dark theme on the dark background. It
shows the logo or brand tile with the application name, a larger heading, a full-width Sign in button, and
the hints (directory accounts, lost administrator access) under a rule at the bottom of the card. The
two-factor step uses the same card, with a larger code field and the recovery-code and "someone else" links
in the card's foot. The first-run setup, e-mail entry and two-factor enrolment pages use the same card.

No URL, permission, API or sign-in behaviour changes.
