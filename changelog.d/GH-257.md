### Security: API tokens stop while their owner's identity provider is disabled

Disabling an identity provider ended the sessions of its accounts, but the API tokens those
accounts owned kept working ([GH#257]). A token whose owner signs in through a disabled provider is
now refused with `401 UNAUTHENTICATED` and recorded as a `token.use` row with outcome
`provider_disabled`. The tokens are not revoked: they work again once the provider is enabled,
as for a disabled account.

**Upgrade:** nothing to do. Integrations that use a token owned by an account of a disabled
provider stop working; give them a token owned by a local service account instead.

[GH#257]: https://github.com/Shadoukita/ShadouCMDB/issues/257
