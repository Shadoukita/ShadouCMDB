### Added: in-app notification actions on workflows, delivered by the action outbox

Administrators can now attach **notification actions** to a workflow. An action puts an entry in the
in-app notifications of chosen profiles, groups or users when a transition runs, an approval request is
made, moves to its next step, closes or becomes overdue, or an instance is cancelled or forced. Actions
belong to the workflow, not to a version: a change applies at once, without publishing, and is audited
as an `update` of the workflow with the actions before and after. E-mail and webhook actions come in a
later release.

- New endpoints: `GET` and `PUT /api/v1/admin/workflow-definitions/{id}/actions` (the set is replaced
  as a whole, like the approvers, with lint warnings in `problems`), and
  `GET /api/v1/admin/workflow-definitions/{id}/actions/{key}/preview?ciId=`, which shows who an action
  would notify now and why anyone is left out (`inactive`, `no_view`, `truncated`). They need
  `workflows.manage` and the view right on the workflow's types, like the other workflow settings.
- **Nothing is sent from inside a transaction.** The transition's own transaction queues one run per
  matching action. After commit, the action workers of each server process resolve the recipients,
  check that each one may view the CI's type, and write the notifications. A recipient who may not view
  it gets nothing and is recorded as skipped (`no_view`). A user who already got the built-in
  notification about the same event is not notified twice.
- **Several server processes** on one database may all run the workers. Runs and deliveries are
  claimed with leases, so each is handled once, and the work of a process that stops mid-way is taken
  over without duplicates when its lease ends.
- **Bounded queue and loop breakers.** Past `WORKFLOW_ACTIONS_QUEUE_MAX` pending items, past
  `WORKFLOW_ACTIONS_MAX_PER_INSTANCE_PER_HOUR` events on one instance, or when the last 10 transitions
  of an instance were all caused by our own deliveries (a client sends `X-ShadouCMDB-Cause: <delivery
  id>`), new runs are kept as `suppressed` with the reason instead of being queued. Each case writes a
  `workflow.action_suppressed` audit entry on the workflow, at most one a minute per workflow and
  reason, with the count.
- Deliveries that fail are retried with exponential backoff (30 s doubling, at most 1 h, honouring
  `Retry-After`) up to `WORKFLOW_ACTIONS_MAX_ATTEMPTS`. A permanent failure, the last attempt, and a
  delivery older than `WORKFLOW_ACTIONS_MAX_AGE_HOURS` are dead, and each dead delivery writes a
  `workflow.action_dead` audit entry (actor system). This applies to the e-mail and webhook channels
  of the later release; in-app notifications are delivered when the run is fanned out.
- In-app notifications gain the kind `workflow_action`.
- New settings, all optional, in `.env.example`: `WORKFLOW_ACTIONS_WORKER`,
  `WORKFLOW_ACTIONS_CONCURRENCY`, `WORKFLOW_ACTIONS_POLL_MS`, `WORKFLOW_ACTIONS_MAX_ATTEMPTS`,
  `WORKFLOW_ACTIONS_MAX_RECIPIENTS`, `WORKFLOW_ACTIONS_QUEUE_MAX`,
  `WORKFLOW_ACTIONS_MAX_PER_INSTANCE_PER_HOUR`, `WORKFLOW_ACTIONS_MAX_AGE_HOURS`,
  `WORKFLOW_ACTIONS_RETENTION_DAYS` and `WORKFLOW_ACTIONS_DEAD_RETENTION_DAYS`.

**Upgrade:** migration 0075 replaces the queueing trigger function and adds indexes on the (still
empty) outbox tables; it rewrites no table. Set `WORKFLOW_ACTIONS_MAX_PER_INSTANCE_PER_HOUR` to the
same value on every server process. Upgrade every replica before you configure the first action.
