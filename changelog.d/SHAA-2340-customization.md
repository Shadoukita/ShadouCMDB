### Changed: Administration › Customization in the new look

The Customization page now has the same header as a CI page. The breadcrumb, the title with the current version,
the introduction and the section tabs (Branding, Navigation, Dashboard, List views, Layouts, History) sit on one band,
with the tabs on its lower edge. The tables in the sections use the same headers as the other lists, the version
history and the class list in Layouts have numbered pages, and the Current, Archived and Unsaved labels are pills.
