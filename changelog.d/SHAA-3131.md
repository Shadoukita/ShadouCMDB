### Added: View a CI as of a past moment, and compare two moments, on its History tab

A CI's **History** tab can now switch from **Timeline** to **As of** and **Compare**. **As of** shows the
CI's fields as they were at a chosen date and time (UTC), clearly marked as historical and read-only, with
**Back to current state**. **Compare** shows the net difference per field between two moments, plus the
workflow steps in that range. Each timeline entry offers **View as of**, and two entries can be selected
and compared. Lookups and references show by name.

The view is rebuilt in the browser from the CI's audit history, as `GET /api/v1/audit-log` shows it to
the reader. It never guesses:

- a reference into a class the reader may not view shows as "hidden";
- a value no audit entry records shows as "not recorded";
- a moment before the CI was created says so;
- a moment before the cutoff of an audit retention purge (`shadoucmdb prune-audit --scope changes`)
  says that the history before it was purged.

At most 2,000 audit entries are read back for one view: beyond that it stops and says so. Relationships
keep their own history and are not part of this view. No API or database change.
