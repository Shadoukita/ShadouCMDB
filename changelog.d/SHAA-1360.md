### Fixed: Business service delete waits for the nesting check; badges and filter checkboxes

**Delete business service** can no longer be confirmed before the dialog has checked which other
business services include the service, or after that check failed. While the check runs the button
stays disabled. If the check fails (for example, the server is busy) the dialog shows the error with
**Retry**, and the button is enabled only once the check succeeds and the dialog states how many
services the service is removed from (GH#477).

The state and criticality badges in the business service tables (**Active**, **Inactive**,
**Deleted**, **Not set**) and their dates now follow the interface language (GH#478). On the
business service list, the **My services** and **Include inactive services** checkboxes now sit
directly beside their labels (GH#479).
